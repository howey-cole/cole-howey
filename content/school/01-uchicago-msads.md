# University of Chicago

## MS, Applied Data Science (started Autumn 2026, expected March 2028)

Cole is completing the degree part time, two courses per quarter, while working full time. Most courses are taught in Python.

### Course plan

The program has six core courses, a two-quarter capstone and four electives.

- **Autumn 2026 (September – December 2026):** Statistical Models for Data Science; Leadership & Consulting for Data Science
- **Winter 2027 (January – March 2027):** Time Series Analysis and Forecasting; Machine Learning I
- **Spring 2027 (March – June 2027):** Machine Learning II; Cloud-Native Data Engineering
- **Summer 2027 (June – August 2027):** two electives. Planned: Causal Models for Data Science; Bayesian Machine Learning with GenAI Applications
- **Autumn 2027 (September – December 2027):** Capstone I; one elective. Planned: Machine Learning Operations
- **Winter 2028 (January – March 2028):** Capstone II; one elective. Planned: Applied Gen AI: Agents & Multimodal Intelligence

The elective choices are Cole's current plan. Elective offerings vary by quarter, so the plan can change.
