# Dick's Sporting Goods

## Sr. Manager of Data Analytics (October 2024 – present)

Cole leads the analytics team that acts as internal consultants to Operations, Technology and Finance. The work covers store operations, online order fulfillment from stores, and the supply chain from distribution centers to stores. He grew the team from one analyst to five, wrote the hiring documentation, and coaches analysts on methods and stakeholder communication.

### Experimentation

- Designs A/B tests of operational processes across the store fleet.
- Simulates the minimum detectable effect before a test starts, and builds sensitivity analysis that estimates return on investment with confidence intervals.
- Analyzes tests with fixed-effects regression in R.
- Test results have informed leadership decisions on whether to roll out an initiative, including decisions not to roll out initiatives that did not show a positive return.

### Causal modeling and root-cause analysis

- Builds generalized linear models, including Gamma GLMs with interaction terms, to explain why an operational or ecommerce metric changed when a controlled test is not possible.
- This work has identified the date a metric shifted and the cause behind it, which allowed the responsible team to correct it.

### Machine learning

- Cole built the team's first store segmentation with principal component analysis (PCA) and K-means clustering in R.
- An analyst on his team later rebuilt it in Python to run on Databricks, with the code versioned in GitHub.
- The segmentation replaced manual, subjective store classification and is used across several store-tiering decisions.

### Metric and data governance

- Designed a new units-per-hour (UPH) productivity metric for ship-from-store fulfillment. His team analyzed timestamp data to derive the event flags the metric needs, software engineers built those flags into the fulfillment product, and Cole's team directed the data cleaning.
- Partnered with Data Engineering on the event services that deliver this data into Databricks, where it feeds governed tables for analytics and reporting.
- Co-led an enterprise RFID inventory data model on Azure Databricks with Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering and Product Management, translating business requirements into governed metrics and reusable data products.
- Regularly presents new metrics and data designs to senior leaders.

### AI-enabled analytics

- Represented analytics in building Databricks Genie Spaces for natural-language, self-service analytics. The company's data organization engineered the tables. Cole's team defined the business logic and trusted table joins, validated the LLM-generated SQL against governed reports, and refined each space's context to reduce incorrect answers.
- Partnered with engineering on a demonstration to senior executives.

### Business intelligence

- Stood up enterprise Power BI reporting that gives field leaders across more than 700 stores a consolidated view of their goal metrics.
- Replaced manual Excel executive reports with automated Power BI reports.

### Tools

SQL, R, Python, Azure Databricks, Google BigQuery, Power BI, GitHub.
