# Lockheed Martin

## Program Management Intern, JAGM Missile Program (part time, 2019)

Through the UCF/Lockheed Martin College Work Experience Program (CWEP), Cole worked about 20 hours a week on the Joint Air-to-Ground Missile (JAGM) program while he was a full-time civil engineering student and a member of the Air National Guard. The work was on a deliverable-based engineering services contract with a U.S. Government customer.

- Coordinated 10 engineering task leaders to deliver the weekly contract status report to the government customer.
- Tracked the program timeline and deliverables against contractual milestones.
- Coordinated communication across engineering, finance and the government customer.
- Created content for VP-level briefings.
