# U.S. Air Force

## Active duty (August 2012 – December 2017)

Cole enlisted at 18 and served about five and a half years on active duty in structural maintenance, a construction trade. He separated as a Staff Sergeant.

- Did hands-on construction and facilities work at Eielson Air Force Base (Alaska), Daegu (South Korea) and Malmstrom Air Force Base (Montana).
- Earned an early promotion at 21, three years ahead of schedule.
- Moved into construction project management for about the last two years of his enlistment. He managed projects from scoping and scheduling through crew coordination and completion, on an indefinite-delivery contract.
- Directly managed two Airmen for about a year and a half.

## Air National Guard (through May 2020)

Cole continued to serve in the Air National Guard while he completed his civil engineering degree and worked part time at Lockheed Martin through the UCF/Lockheed Martin College Work Experience Program.
