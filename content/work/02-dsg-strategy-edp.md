## Sr. Manager of Strategy, Executive Development Program (September 2023 – October 2024)

Cole joined Dick's Sporting Goods through its Executive Development Program, a rotational leadership program for MBA graduates housed in the Strategy department. The projects were strategy-oriented: market sizing, idea generation and business case development.

- **Marketing Personalization rotation:** ran A/B tests on direct marketing channels: SMS, push notifications and email.
- **Loss Prevention rotation:** coordinated a multi-tactic pilot across operations, finance, analytics and loss prevention, tracked deployment against the measurement plan, and informed the rollout strategy.
- **Opportunity sizing:** built a SQL cohort analysis that measured customer spend relative to the date customers adopted a service, then proposed and won support for adding the opportunity to the Technology roadmap.
